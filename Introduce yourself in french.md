# 🇫🇷 Se présenter et présenter quelqu'un (FLE A1)

A French as a Foreign Language (FLE) teaching resource designed for **A1 beginners**. This lesson helps learners introduce themselves and introduce other people using simple French expressions and essential grammar.

---

## 📖 Overview

This lesson enables students to:

- Introduce themselves
- Introduce another person
- Ask for and give personal information
- Communicate during a first meeting

The lesson includes practical vocabulary, grammar explanations, dialogues, activities, and verb conjugations. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, learners will be able to:

- Say their name
- Give their age
- State their nationality
- Talk about their profession
- Say where they live
- Ask someone for personal information
- Introduce another person

---

## 📚 Lesson Content

### ✨ Vocabulary

- Je m'appelle...
- J'ai ... ans
- Je suis...
- J'habite à...
- Comment tu t'appelles ?
- Quel âge as-tu ?
- Quelle est ta nationalité ?
- Quelle est ta profession ?
- Où habites-tu ?

---

### 📝 Grammar

The lesson explains how to use:

- **S'appeler**
- **Être**
- **Avoir**
- **Habiter**

It also provides sentence structures for:

- Introducing yourself
- Introducing someone else
- Asking personal questions

---

## 💬 Sample Dialogue

```text
Bonjour !

Comment tu t'appelles ?
Je m'appelle Abele.

Quel âge as-tu ?
J'ai 30 ans.

Quelle est ta nationalité ?
Je suis Nigérian(e).

Quelle est ta profession ?
Je suis une chanteuse.

Tu habites où ?
J'habite au numéro 3 rue Ade, à Rumuokwuta.
```
